Accessed October 5, 2026. Selection was purposive: conceptual distinctions,
selection, cumulative advantage, causal identification, and probabilistic
evaluation. It was not exhaustive or a preregistered systematic search. No
paywalled full text is claimed as read when only an abstract was available.

The binomial and beta-binomial arithmetic, conditional-normal calculation, urn
equivalence, clustered variance derivation, and binary Brier decomposition in
the essays are calculations developed for this series. They are not empirical
results borrowed from these papers. The proposed seven-commitment framework is
the author's synthesis, not a framework attributed to any one source.

The archive includes references and our own materials only, not copies of these
papers. Additional sources located but not substantively read are excluded from
the argument rather than used to enlarge the bibliography.
