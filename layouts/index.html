Music, embeddings, and narrative arcs

How does music develop an idea?

I explore how a band's lyrics change and how songs relate through close reading, embeddings, and narrative-arc analysis. A recurring theme can change its meaning; an album's ending can reshape how we hear its beginning.

The essays examine the Beatles' lyrical evolution, multilingual lyrics in Aquamosh, and what an agent needs to remember across an album. Each experiment describes the evidence it uses and the limits of its interpretation.
