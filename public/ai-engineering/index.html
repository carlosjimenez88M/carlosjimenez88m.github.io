AI Software Engineering

Building AI systems that can be evaluated, understood, and operated in production.

A model is one component of an AI application. The work around it includes context, interfaces, tools, evaluation, infrastructure, and the decisions that determine how it behaves when something fails.

That is one of the two primary lines of this blog. I write about the engineering needed to make those systems observable, testable, and useful under real constraints.

Suggested starting points#

  • MLflow for AI Engineering: Prompts Are Release Artifacts — version prompts, evaluate changes, inspect traces, and make controlled releases.
  • Anatomy of an MLOps Pipeline, Part 1 — orchestration and reproducible pipelines; continue with deployment and production.
  • AI Architecture: Training and Inference — hardware and the operational constraints around inference.
  • Statistical Learning: Foundations — estimation, bias, and variance behind the evaluation work.

For constrained hardware and autonomous workflows, see Edge + Agentic AI. The other primary line is narrative arcs in music: how songs and albums develop ideas, and what computational methods can tell us about that structure.
