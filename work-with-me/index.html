Music, embeddings, and narrative arcs

My independent music research explores how ideas recur and change across songs, albums, and a band’s discography. I use embeddings and NLP to study relationships between lyrics, then return to listening and close reading to examine what those relationships mean. I also study narrative arcs in literature: how a sequence develops an idea, and how a later passage changes our interpretation of an earlier one.

These questions shape my experiments on representation, memory, and verification. What does a representation preserve? Which detail must a system retrieve to support an interpretation? Where does a confident reading outrun the evidence?

The public work linked here consists of research studies and educational examples, with their own methods and limitations. They are not client results. Applying a finding to your system requires its own evaluation.

Before we start

How is pricing agreed?

I quote each engagement after we define the question, scope, deliverables, and access requirements. We agree on the price before the work starts; a diagnostic, architecture review, and ongoing guidance have their own scopes.

What should I prepare or share?

Start with a non-sensitive description and an example you are authorized to share. If we proceed, we agree on the representative cases, traces, outputs, and source material required, along with appropriate access.

Does this include implementation?

These engagements focus on evaluation, review, and technical decisions. Production integrations, large-scale data preparation, and implementation work need a separate agreed scope. Improvements in quality or cost are outcomes to measure.
